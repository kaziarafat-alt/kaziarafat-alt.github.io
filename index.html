What I've learned so far

Three lessons, learned on the job.

Lesson 01

Marketing

Know who the customer is, say one clear thing, and say it where they already spend time.

How I apply it

  1. Research the audience
  2. Plan a content calendar
  3. Make Reels and creatives
  4. Run and read Meta Ads

Lesson 02

Sales

Clients need to trust you before anything else. Listening carefully matters more than pitching.

How I apply it

  1. Understand the client's need
  2. Turn it into a clear brief
  3. Present it simply
  4. Deliver and stay in touch

Lesson 03

Promotion

People trust people. Creators and real events help a brand feel closer to its audience.

How I apply it

  1. Choose the right creators
  2. Share it online
  3. Support on-ground activations
  4. Help visitors become customers
