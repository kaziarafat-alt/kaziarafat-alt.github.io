How to use
  1. Open this week's tab. Do the session for today's date.
  2. After each exercise, type the kg and reps of your best set.
  3. Tap Mark done at the end. It saves itself.
  4. See your records and improvement in Progress.

Techniques

  • SS superset: 2 exercises back to back, no rest between.
  • DS drop set: at failure, drop weight 25%, keep going (2 drops).
  • RP rest-pause: at failure, rest 15 s, do more reps, repeat.
  • MR myo-reps: 1 set of 12–15, then 4 mini-sets of 3–5.
  • 21s: 7 bottom-half, 7 top-half, 7 full reps.
  • ISO hold the squeeze 2 s. ECC lower in 4 s.

Use techniques on the last set only.

Rules

  • Warm up 8–10 min, then 2 light sets of the first lift.
  • Hit the top of the rep range on all sets → add 1–2.5 kg (upper) or 2.5–5 kg (legs) next time.
  • RIR 2 = stop when 2 more reps are left.
  • Missed a day? Do it the next day. Never 2 sessions in a day.
  • Sharp joint pain: stop and swap the exercise.

Food & health

  • 150 g protein a day: eggs, chicken, fish, dal, doi, whey.
  • Calories: shown on each week. Water 3–4 L.
  • Almost no sugar, fried food or cold drinks.
  • Creatine 3–5 g daily.
  • Kegels: hold 5 s, release 5 s, ×10, 3 times a day.
  • Sleep 7–9 hours.
